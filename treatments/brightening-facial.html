Brightening Facial

Overview

Brightening Facial is focused on bringing back clarity and glow when skin looks tired, uneven, or dull. It combines cleansing, comfortable resurfacing, and hydration layers to help your complexion look more even and polished — without feeling harsh or aggressive.

What To Expect

We begin with a quick skin check-in, then tailor the treatment to your sensitivity and current condition. Expect a thorough cleanse, gentle exfoliation, targeted brightening steps, and a soothing mask, finished with facial massage and hydration. The pacing stays calm, and the finish is clean, smooth, and bright.

Best For

Ideal before events, after travel, or whenever you want your skin to look more awake and even. Great for dullness and uneven tone, especially if you prefer a balanced, comfort-first facial.

Aftercare

Keep the rest of your routine minimal: gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and SPF. Avoid strong actives and additional exfoliation for 24–48 hours, and let the skin settle. If you want to maintain the brightness, we can recommend a simple routine to support it at home.
